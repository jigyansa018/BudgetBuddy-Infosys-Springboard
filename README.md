Abstract

In the dynamic landscape of personal finance, effective budget planning and expense management are crucial for helping students control their spending, achieve financial goals, and make informed financial decisions. BudgetBuddy is an intelligent student budget planning and personal expense management platform designed to track income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, notifications, and financial reports in an organized manner. The project follows a structured approach involving user profile management, income and expense tracking, budget planning, savings goal monitoring, threshold-based alerts, and financial analytics. By analyzing spending patterns, budget utilization, income, and savings progress, the platform provides meaningful insights that help users understand their financial behavior and take appropriate actions. The system aims to support students in maintaining better spending habits, identifying unnecessary expenses, staying within planned budgets, and achieving their savings objectives. Through data-driven insights, personalized notifications, and financial reports, BudgetBuddy contributes to improved financial awareness, disciplined money management, and smarter day-to-day financial decision-making.
